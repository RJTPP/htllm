## Weak or Implicit Rationale

1. **Image compression target (256KB):** Carried from Phase 1. Still no empirical validation against WebLLM vision models. Risk is low — working in practice but not formally tested.

2. **CSP `'unsafe-inline'` for styles:** Carried from Phase 1. No exploration of nonce-based alternatives with Astro's build pipeline. Low risk for styles-only.

3. **`https://*` wildcard in CSP connect-src:** Required for BYOK cloud providers but very permissive. The SDD documents this as intentional with user-trust boundary, but could be tightened per-provider if the registry stored endpoint URLs at build time. Currently acceptable for flexibility.

4. **Default accent color selection:** Currently amber (Phase 1 default). Eye-strain research suggests cooler tones are better for sustained dark-theme use. Decision is explicitly listed as unresolved in Section 10.
